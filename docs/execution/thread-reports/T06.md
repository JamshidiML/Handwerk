## Delivered Behavior

- Critical rules cover wall area, wall coats, ceiling inclusion and conditional area, substrate soundness, and conditional repair scope.
- Missing, explicitly unknown, uncertain, contradictory, and stale states stay unresolved until a valid human answer or authoritative fact exists.
- Question and answer IDs, evidence fingerprints, exclusions, and readiness are deterministic for identical inputs.
- Citation factories produce revision-aware locators for transcript segments, measurements, user answers, company rules, and photo context.
- Commercial provenance requires at least one authoritative source; photo context always remains context-only.
- React exports provide native answer controls, readiness announcements, stale-answer presentation, `Warum diese Position?`, exclusion notices, and confirmed/inferred/edited/excluded/unresolved states.
